01 — Who we are

A lab,
not just an agency

Most software companies build exactly what you ask for and then move on. We start by asking why. We study the problem, test the idea on real people, and only then start building.

The result is software that solves the real problem — not just the first version of it you described. It also means fewer expensive surprises later.

We started in 2024 in Pakistan. Since then we have delivered projects for startups, growing brands and established businesses across the UK, Pakistan and beyond — and built seven products of our own along the way.
